History



The School of Computer Engineering (SoCE) at the Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT), Manipal
was established in 2025 by merging the long-standing departments of Computer Science & Engineering and Information Technology under a unified academic and research umbrella.

MIT itself was founded in 1957 by T. M. A. Pai as one of India's first self-financing engineering colleges.

Evolution and Milestones

  • 1957: Manipal Institute of Technology is established in Manipal, Karnataka.

  • 1993: MIT becomes a constituent college of the newly formed Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE),
    which was granted deemed-to-be-university status.

  • Decades of Computing Growth: Computer-related courses expanded from early foundational programming and hardware labs into diverse streams including Data Science,
    Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Cyber Security.

  • 2025: The School of Computer Engineering (SoCE) is formally organized to synergize faculty expertise, graduate programs,
    and research labs (such as cybersecurity and AI-driven healthcare initiatives).

Focus and Research Areas

  • Integrates core computational teachings with specialized tracks like Quantum Computing, Computer Vision, IoT, and Cloud/Edge Computing.

  • Houses active collaborations and centers of excellence alongside industry leaders like Schneider Electric and national AI missions.
