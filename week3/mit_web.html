1. Introduction

The School of Computer Engineering at MIT, MAHE, brings together faculty from the fields of Computer Science, Information and Communication Technology, Data Science, and Computer Applications.

The school provides a rich academic environment with a focus on:

  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • High-Performance Computing
  • Networks and Security
  • Data Science
  • Multimedia Systems

The school focuses on teaching excellence, innovation, research, experiential learning, international collaboration, and industry interaction.

2. History

The School of Computer Engineering at the Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT), Manipal, was established in 2025.

It was formed by merging the long-standing departments of Computer Science & Engineering and Information Technology under a unified academic and research structure.

MIT was founded in 1957 by Dr. T. M. A. Pai and is one of India's early self-financing engineering institutions.  Learn More

6. About MAHE

Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) is an Institution of Eminence Deemed to be University and a self-financing higher educational institution.

MAHE began its operations in 1953 with the establishment of its first self-financing medical college.

Over the years, several institutions were added to the group. In June 1993, the Government of India declared the group as a Deemed to be University.

In October 2020, MAHE was declared an Institution of Eminence (IoE) Deemed to be University by the Ministry of Education, Government of India.
